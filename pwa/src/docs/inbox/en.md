# Inbox

## Purpose

Quickly record your ideas and categorize them later.

## How to Use

1. Add:
   1. Click `+Add` to add a new item.
   2. On iPhone, you can bind the `NBL Inbox` shortcut to `Settings ➡️ Accessibility ➡️ Touch ➡️ Back Tap ➡️ Double Tap`. This way, double-tapping the back of your phone will automatically add a new idea.
   3. On any webpage on iPhone, after installing `NBL Inbox`, you can also share the URL to NonBlockingLife's Inbox.
   4. On Android, you can add the `NBL Inbox` widget to the home screen for quick idea recording.
   5. The `Share` feature in YouTube, Chrome, etc., can also be used to share content to NonBlockingLife's Inbox.
2. Delete:
   1. Each record has a `Delete` button, or you can swipe left on mobile.
   2. Find time each day to categorize, and delete after categorizing.
      1. Delete trivial tasks as soon as they are done.
      2. For minor tasks without strict deadlines, copy the title and add it to `Micro Tasks`.
      3. For time-related tasks, add them to `Scheduled`.
      4. For tasks that take more than a day, add them to `Task Pool`.
      5. For reference materials, please use your preferred external storage resources, such as `OneNote`, `Notion`, etc.
3. Edit:
    1. On mobile, swipe the item to the right to enter the edit screen, and press `Save` after making changes.
    2. On a computer, changes are updated as soon as the focus leaves the input field.
